Automated Attendance Check-in

No manual interaction is required. Once the teacher activates the classroom camera scan, your attendance is recorded automatically when you are detected by the camera. Just enter and remain in the classroom as normal — there is no button to press.

  1. Enter and remain in the classroom as normal. No login or manual check-in action is required.
  2. When the teacher triggers a check-in, the classroom camera captures the faces present and the system runs the SCRFD + ArcFace recognition pipeline against the registered facial registry.
  3. The system records your attendance status for that check-in automatically.
  4. The teacher may trigger additional check-ins during the session to verify continued presence; your status is updated accordingly.
  5. At the end of the session the system assigns your final status (Present / Late / Left Early / Absent). You can review it under My Attendance, and you will be notified if marked Late or Absent.
